Privacy Policy

Registered Company Declaration — Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) Registration: BN2687328 — Azpeters Consults is the parent company of UghelliRides.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

UghelliRides ("we", "us", "our") is a service of Azpeters Consults. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Information you submit — name, phone number, email, address, and details about vehicles or driver credentials, when you use our registration or booking forms.
  • Booking information — pickup location, destination, time, and service preferences.
  • Usage data — via cookies/analytics (only with your consent): pages visited, approximate location, and device type.

2. How we use your data

  • To process bookings and registrations.
  • To verify driver and vehicle credentials.
  • To contact you about your request.
  • To improve our website and services (aggregate, non-identifying analytics).

3. Cookies & analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand site usage. Tracking only activates after you accept the cookie banner. You may decline — the site works fully either way. IP addresses are anonymised.

4. Legal basis (NDPR / GDPR)

  • Consent — for analytics cookies.
  • Legitimate interest — to operate and improve the service.
  • Contract / pre-contract — to fulfil your booking or registration request.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with: our analytics provider (Google, anonymised), and payment processors when you transact. No other third parties.

6. Data retention

  • Registrations and bookings — as long as needed to operate, or as required by law.
  • Analytics — up to 14 months (Google's default), then auto-deleted.

7. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, or portability of your data. Contact us and we respond within 30 days.
