Our Booking Process

Booking with UghelliRides is designed to be simple and transparent. Here's exactly what happens when you reach out:

  1. Initial Contact: Tell us your pickup location, destination, date, time, and vehicle preference (Economy, SUV, Executive, Luxury, or Group)
  2. Quote & Availability: We provide a fixed, all-inclusive price within 15 minutes — no hidden fees, no meter surprises
  3. Confirmation: Once you approve the quote, we secure your vehicle and assign a vetted chauffeur
  4. Details Shared: You receive the driver's name, contact number, vehicle details, and license plate before pickup
  5. Service Delivery: Your chauffeur arrives 10 minutes early, assists with luggage, and ensures a smooth, comfortable journey
  6. Post-Trip: We follow up for feedback and provide an invoice if needed for reimbursement or accounting
