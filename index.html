Questions, answered.

What do I need to rent a car?
A valid ID (National ID, driver's licence or passport) plus a refundable deposit. Chauffeur-driven additionally requires a valid driver's licence.
Is fuel included?
Chauffeur-driven rates include fuel within Ughelli and environs. Chauffeur-driven cars come with a full tank and should be returned full. Interstate trips are quoted with fuel included.
Do you travel outside Delta State?
Yes — Warri, Asaba, Benin, Port Harcourt, Lagos and beyond. Interstate fares are quoted per destination.
How do owners and drivers earn?
Register annually (vehicles / drivers), complete verification, and matched bookings begin immediately after approval. Payouts land weekly.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 6 hours before pickup. Later cancellations attract 20%. No-shows are charged 50% of the fare.
